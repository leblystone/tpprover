## 🚨 Most Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not deploying changes** - Make sure robots.txt and index.html are live!
2. **Not submitting to Search Console** - This is CRITICAL, don't skip it
3. **Expecting immediate results** - AI search takes 2-4 weeks
4. **Not getting backlinks** - Post on Reddit, Product Hunt, social media
5. **Not testing regularly** - Test weekly to track progress
6. **Giving up too soon** - Be patient, this is a marathon not a sprint
