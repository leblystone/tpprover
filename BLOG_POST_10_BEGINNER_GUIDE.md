## 🧬 PEPTIDES 101: WHAT ARE THEY?

### **THE SIMPLE EXPLANATION:**

Peptides are **short chains of amino acids** (the building blocks of proteins).

They're like **text messages to your body** telling it to:
- Burn fat (GLP-1s)
- Heal injuries (BPC-157, TB-500)
- Build muscle (CJC, Ipamorelin)
- Improve cognition (Semax, Selank)
- Support longevity (MOTS-C, Epithalon)

---

### **WHY PEOPLE RESEARCH PEPTIDES:**

✅ **Targeted effects** (specific outcomes)
✅ **Fewer side effects** (than many alternatives)
✅ **Natural to the body** (many mimic hormones you already have)
✅ **Research-backed** (growing body of studies)

## 🧪 STEP 4: RECONSTITUTION (MIXING YOUR PEPTIDE)

### **THE PROCESS (DON'T PANIC - IT'S EASY):**

**1. GATHER SUPPLIES**
- Peptide vial (lyophilized)
- Bacteriostatic water
- Syringe
- Alcohol wipes

**2. CLEAN EVERYTHING**
- Wipe rubber stoppers with alcohol

**3. DRAW BAC WATER**
- Use calculator to determine amount needed
- [Use our reconstitution calculator →](#)
- **Example:** 2mL BAC water for 5mg vial

**4. INJECT WATER INTO PEPTIDE VIAL**
- **SLOWLY** inject down the side of vial (NOT directly on powder)
- Never shake - swirl gently until dissolved

**5. LABEL THE VIAL**
- Peptide name + mg
- BAC water amount
- Date mixed
- **EXPIRATION DATE** (30 days later)

**6. STORE IN FRIDGE**
- Back of fridge (not door)
- Away from light

---

### **RECONSTITUTION CALCULATOR:**

**FORMULA:**
```
(Total mg ÷ Total mL BAC water) × Desired dose = Injection volume
```

**EXAMPLE:**
- 5mg vial + 2mL BAC water
- Want 0.25mg dose
- (5mg ÷ 2mL) × 0.25mg = 0.1mL (10 units on syringe)

**💡 USE A CALCULATOR** - Don't guess!

## 💉 STEP 5: YOUR FIRST INJECTION

### **INJECTION BASICS:**

**SUBCUTANEOUS (MOST COMMON):**
- Under the skin, into fat layer
- Common sites: abdomen, thighs, upper arms
- Least painful, easiest for beginners

**INTRAMUSCULAR (SOME PEPTIDES):**
- Into muscle tissue
- Common sites: deltoid, glutes, thighs
- Required for some peptides (check protocol)

---

### **FIRST INJECTION STEP-BY-STEP:**

**1. PREPARE YOUR WORKSPACE**
- Clean surface
- Gather: peptide vial, syringe, alcohol wipes

**2. WASH YOUR HANDS**

**3. DRAW YOUR DOSE**
- Clean vial stopper with alcohol
- Insert needle, draw prescribed dose
- Check for air bubbles (tap syringe, push air out)

**4. CLEAN INJECTION SITE**
- Use alcohol wipe
- Let dry (30 seconds)

**5. PINCH SKIN (for subcutaneous)**
- Create a small fold of skin
- Insert needle at 45-90° angle

**6. INJECT SLOWLY**
- Push plunger slowly (over 5-10 seconds)
- Release skin pinch

**7. REMOVE NEEDLE**
- Pull straight out
- Press alcohol wipe on site (don't rub)

**8. DISPOSE OF NEEDLE**
- Place in sharps container immediately
- **NEVER recap needles**

**9. LOG YOUR DOSE**
- Time, amount, injection site
- [Track in The Pep Planner →](https://thepepplanner.com/shop/p/monthly-pep-planner-app)

---

### **FIRST INJECTION NERVES:**

**IT'S NORMAL TO BE NERVOUS.**

**TIPS:**
- ✅ Watch YouTube videos first (search "subcutaneous injection tutorial")
- ✅ Use insulin syringes (tiny needles, barely feel it)
- ✅ Inject slower (rushing makes it worse)
- ✅ Breathe (holding breath = more tension)
- ✅ Ice injection site first (numbs skin)

**After 2-3 injections, it becomes routine.**

## 📊 STEP 6: START LOW, GO SLOW

### **THE GOLDEN RULE:**

**ALWAYS start at the LOWEST recommended dose.**

**WHY:**
- Assess tolerance
- Minimize side effects
- Build gradually
- Safer approach

---

### **ESCALATION EXAMPLE (SEMAGLUTIDE):**

**DON'T DO THIS:**
- Week 1: Jump to 1mg (too high, too fast)

**DO THIS:**
- Weeks 1-4: 0.25mg (assess tolerance)
- Weeks 5-8: 0.5mg (first increase)
- Weeks 9-12: 1mg (continue escalation)

**⏰ BE PATIENT.** Results compound over time.

## ⚠️ STEP 8: KNOW THE RED FLAGS

### **WHEN TO STOP OR ADJUST:**

**🚨 STOP IMMEDIATELY IF:**
- Severe allergic reaction (hives, difficulty breathing)
- Extreme pain at injection site (infection signs)
- Vision changes (rare but serious)
- Severe pancreatitis symptoms (intense abdominal pain)

**🟡 ADJUST IF:**
- Side effects are unbearable (reduce dose)
- No results after 4-6 weeks (reassess protocol)
- Injection site reactions persist (rotate sites more)

---

### **WHEN TO CONSULT A HEALTHCARE PROVIDER:**

- Pre-existing medical conditions
- Taking other medications
- Unsure about side effects
- Planning surgery (some peptides affect healing)

## 🚨 BEGINNER MISTAKES TO AVOID

### **MISTAKE #1: STARTING WITH TOO MANY PEPTIDES**

**DON'T:** Stack 3-4 peptides on Day 1

**DO:** Start with ONE peptide, assess response, add more later (if needed)

---

### **MISTAKE #2: SKIPPING RECONSTITUTION MATH**

**DON'T:** Eyeball your doses

**DO:** Use a calculator, measure precisely

---

### **MISTAKE #3: NOT ROTATING INJECTION SITES**

**DON'T:** Inject same spot every time

**DO:** Rotate through quadrants (prevents scar tissue)

---

### **MISTAKE #4: GIVING UP TOO EARLY**

**DON'T:** Quit after 1 week because "nothing's happening"

**DO:** Commit to 4-6 weeks minimum (peptides take time)

---

### **MISTAKE #5: BUYING FROM SKETCHY VENDORS**

**DON'T:** Buy based on price alone

**DO:** Check reviews, verify COAs, compare cost per mg
