## THE SCAR TISSUE PROBLEM

You've been injecting peptides for 3 months.

**Same routine:**
- Right side of abdomen
- About 2 inches from belly button
- Same spot, every single time

**Month 4:**

Injections start hurting more. The site feels hard. Absorption seems slower.

**THE REALIZATION:**

You've built up scar tissue from repeatedly injecting the same spot.

Now your peptide:
- Absorbs poorly (wasted money)
- Hurts to inject (makes you dread dosing)
- Requires more force to inject

**All because you didn't track injection sites.**

Here's how to fix it (and prevent it).

---

## 🎯 WHY INJECTION SITE TRACKING MATTERS

### **THE PROBLEMS WITH POOR ROTATION:**

❌ **SCAR TISSUE BUILD-UP**
- Repeated trauma to same spot
- Reduces absorption
- Makes injections painful

❌ **INCONSISTENT ABSORPTION**
- Some sites absorb faster than others
- Affects your results
- Hard to identify patterns without tracking

❌ **RUNNING OUT OF SPOTS**
- Forget where you last injected
- Hit same spots too frequently
- Limits usable injection areas

---

### **THE BENEFITS OF GOOD TRACKING:**

✅ **EVEN SITE ROTATION**
- No scar tissue build-up
- Better absorption
- Painless injections

✅ **CONSISTENT RESULTS**
- Predictable absorption rates
- Can identify best sites for each peptide
- Data to optimize your protocol

✅ **NO MORE GUESSING**
- "Where did I inject last time?"
- "When was the last time I used this site?"
- Tracking answers these instantly

## 📍 INJECTION SITE ZONES (THE MAP)

### **COMMON SUBCUTANEOUS SITES:**

**ZONE 1: ABDOMEN**
- Most common site
- Easiest to reach
- Good absorption
- **Divide into 8 quadrants** (avoid 2" around navel)

**ZONE 2: THIGHS (OUTER/FRONT)**
- Easy to access
- Good for daily injectors
- **Divide each thigh into 4 quadrants**

**ZONE 3: UPPER ARMS (BACK/OUTER)**
- Harder to reach (might need help)
- Good absorption
- **Divide each arm into 2-3 spots**

**ZONE 4: LOVE HANDLES/FLANKS**
- Often overlooked
- Easy pinch
- **Divide each side into 2-3 spots**

**TOTAL USABLE SITES:** 20-30+ spots (if you track properly)

---

## 🔄 ROTATION STRATEGIES

### **STRATEGY #1: QUADRANT ROTATION (MOST COMMON)**

**HOW IT WORKS:**

Divide abdomen into 8 quadrants:
- Upper left
- Upper right
- Mid-left
- Mid-right
- Lower left
- Lower right
- Far left (love handle)
- Far right (love handle)

**ROTATION SCHEDULE:**
- Day 1: Upper left
- Day 2: Upper right
- Day 3: Mid-left
- Day 4: Mid-right
- (continue pattern)

**RULE:** Don't return to same quadrant for 7-14 days

---

### **STRATEGY #2: FULL BODY ROTATION**

**HOW IT WORKS:**

Rotate through ALL available zones:
- Abdomen → Thighs → Arms → Love handles → Repeat

**EXAMPLE (DAILY INJECTOR):**
- Mon: Abdomen (left)
- Tue: Thigh (left)
- Wed: Abdomen (right)
- Thu: Thigh (right)
- Fri: Abdomen (upper left)
- Sat: Love handle (left)
- Sun: Love handle (right)

**RULE:** 7-10 days before returning to exact spot

---

### **STRATEGY #3: SIDE-TO-SIDE ALTERNATION**

**HOW IT WORKS:**

Simple left/right rotation:
- Injection 1: Left abdomen
- Injection 2: Right abdomen
- Injection 3: Left thigh
- Injection 4: Right thigh

**BEST FOR:** Beginners or those with limited sites

**RULE:** Vary exact location within left/right (not same exact spot)

## 🚨 INJECTION SITE MISTAKES

### **MISTAKE #1: ALWAYS INJECTING IN THE EASIEST SPOT**

**THE PROBLEM:**

Right-handed people tend to inject left side of abdomen (easier to reach).

**Result:** Left side gets 90% of injections → scar tissue.

**THE FIX:**

✅ **FORCE YOURSELF** to rotate even if it's less convenient
✅ Track sites (holds you accountable)
✅ Set reminders: "Next injection = right side"

---

### **MISTAKE #2: NOT TRACKING WHICH PEPTIDE GOES WHERE**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You inject 3 different peptides. You forget which one went where today.

**You inject Peptide A in a site you JUST used for Peptide B 2 hours ago.**

**THE FIX:**

✅ Log EVERY injection (peptide name + site)
✅ Use different zones for different peptides (if injecting multiple per day)

---

### **MISTAKE #3: ROTATING TOO SLOWLY**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You have 8 available quadrants but only rotate between 2-3.

**THE FIX:**

✅ **USE ALL AVAILABLE SITES** (abdomen, thighs, love handles, arms)
✅ Expand your comfort zone gradually
✅ More sites = longer rest between repeats = healthier tissue

---

### **MISTAKE #4: INJECTING TOO CLOSE TO LAST SITE**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You rotate "left abdomen" to "right abdomen" but inject 1 inch away from yesterday's spot.

**Still too close → tissue irritation.**

**THE FIX:**

✅ **MINIMUM DISTANCE:** 2-3 inches from previous injection
✅ Divide zones into specific quadrants
✅ Track EXACT locations (not just "left abdomen")

## 💡 SITE-SPECIFIC TIPS

### **ABDOMEN (MOST COMMON):**

✅ **PROS:**
- Easiest to access
- Good fat layer (most people)
- Large surface area

⚠️ **TIPS:**
- Avoid 2" around belly button (more nerve endings)
- Pinch skin to create injection target
- Clean area thoroughly (prone to bacteria)

---

### **THIGHS (OUTER/FRONT):**

✅ **PROS:**
- Easy to reach
- Large surface area
- Good rotation option

⚠️ **TIPS:**
- Outer/front thighs (NOT inner - more nerve endings)
- Sit down when injecting (easier pinch)
- Avoid area right above knee (bony, less fat)

---

### **UPPER ARMS (BACK/OUTER):**

✅ **PROS:**
- Out of sight (less visible injection marks)
- Good absorption

⚠️ **TIPS:**
- Harder to reach (might need mirror or help)
- Back/outer arm only (NOT inner bicep)
- Smaller surface area (fewer spots)

---

### **LOVE HANDLES/FLANKS:**

✅ **PROS:**
- Often forgotten (great for rotation)
- Easy to pinch
- Good fat layer (most people)

⚠️ **TIPS:**
- Side of torso, above hip bone
- Easy self-injection (no mirror needed)
- Great "extra" rotation zone

## 🎯 WHEN TO SKIP A SITE

### **SKIP IF:**

❌ **BRUISING** (wait until healed)
❌ **SORENESS** (give it more rest)
❌ **REDNESS/IRRITATION** (inflammation present)
❌ **LUMP/HARDNESS** (scar tissue forming)
❌ **RECENTLY USED** (< 7 days for daily injectors, < 14 days for weekly)

**MOVE TO NEXT AVAILABLE SITE IN ROTATION.**

## ✅ INJECTION SITE TRACKING CHECKLIST

**BEFORE EACH INJECTION:**

□ Check tracking log (where did I inject last?)
□ Identify next rotation site (follow pattern)
□ Inspect site (any bruising, soreness, lumps?)
□ If site not available, move to next in rotation

**DURING INJECTION:**

□ Clean site thoroughly (alcohol wipe, let dry)
□ Pinch skin properly
□ Note pain level (1-10) and difficulty

**AFTER INJECTION:**

□ Log immediately (date, time, peptide, exact site)
□ Add any notes (pain, difficulty, issues)
□ Plan next injection site
□ Dispose of needle safely
