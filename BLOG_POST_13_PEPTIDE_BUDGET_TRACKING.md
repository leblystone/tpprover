## 🚨 BUDGET KILLERS (WHAT'S WASTING YOUR MONEY)

### **BUDGET KILLER #1: NOT TRACKING COST PER MG**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You buy based on "cheapest vial" not "cheapest per mg."

**EXAMPLE:**

**Vendor A:** 5mg vial for $30 = **$6/mg**
**Vendor B:** 10mg vial for $55 = **$5.50/mg**

You buy from Vendor A because "$30 is cheaper than $55."

**Result:** You're overpaying 9% on every order.

**Over a year:** Wasted $100-200.

**THE FIX:**

✅ **ALWAYS calculate cost per mg**
✅ [Use vendor comparison tracking →](read our vendor guide)

---

### **BUDGET KILLER #2: EXPIRED VIALS**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You reconstitute a vial, use it for 2 weeks, then forget about it.

It expires at 30 days. You toss it.

**You wasted 50% of that vial = $25-50.**

**THE FIX:**

✅ **Label expiration dates clearly**
✅ **Set reminders** (7 days before expiration)
✅ **Track all active vials** ([The Pep Planner app](https://thepepplanner.com/shop/p/monthly-pep-planner-app) auto-alerts you)

---

### **BUDGET KILLER #3: RUSHED SHIPPING**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You run out of peptides mid-protocol because you didn't reorder on time.

**Standard shipping:** $8 (arrives in 7 days)
**Rushed shipping:** $35 (arrives in 2 days)

**You pay $27 extra because you didn't plan ahead.**

**THE FIX:**

✅ **Track remaining doses**
✅ **Reorder at 2-week supply threshold**
✅ **Set low-stock alerts**

---

### **BUDGET KILLER #4: BUYING FROM BAD VENDORS**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You order from a sketchy vendor because they're cheap.

Peptide arrives underdosed or fake. Doesn't work.

**You wasted $50-100 on useless product.**

**THE FIX:**

✅ **Only buy from vetted vendors** (check COAs, reviews)
✅ **Don't chase "too good to be true" prices**
✅ [Read our vendor comparison guide →](#)

## 💡 COST-SAVING STRATEGIES

### **STRATEGY #1: BUY LARGER VIALS**

**EXAMPLE:**

**5mg vial:** $30 ($6/mg)
**10mg vial:** $50 ($5/mg)

**Savings:** $1/mg

**Annual savings:** $100-200 (depending on usage)

**⚠️ CAUTION:** Only if you'll use it all before expiration (30 days reconstituted).

---

### **STRATEGY #2: BATCH ORDERS (REDUCE SHIPPING)**

**INSTEAD OF:**
- Order 1 vial every 3 weeks ($8 shipping × 17/year = $136/year)

**DO THIS:**
- Order 2-3 vials every 6-8 weeks ($8 shipping × 7/year = $56/year)

**Savings:** $80/year

**⚠️ CAUTION:** Only for lyophilized vials (long shelf life). Don't stock up on peptides you haven't tried yet.

---

### **STRATEGY #3: TRACK WASTE & REDUCE IT**

**COMMON WASTE SOURCES:**
- Expired vials: $50-100/year
- Wrong reconstitution (overdosing): $100-200/year
- Mixing up vials (injecting wrong peptide): $50/year
- Dropped/contaminated vials: $50/year

**TOTAL POTENTIAL WASTE:** $250-550/year

**THE FIX:**

✅ Track expiration dates
✅ Use reconstitution calculator
✅ Label vials clearly
✅ Be careful with vials

**Savings:** $250-550/year

---

### **STRATEGY #4: CYCLE PEPTIDES (DON'T RUN EVERYTHING AT ONCE)**

**INSTEAD OF:**
- Running 3-4 peptides simultaneously (**$200-400/month**)

**DO THIS:**
- Focus on 1-2 at a time
- Cycle based on goals (recovery peptide for 8 weeks, then switch to GLP-1)

**Monthly cost:** **$80-150/month**

**Savings:** $1,440-3,000/year

**PLUS:** Simpler protocols, less chaos, better focus.

## 🎯 WHEN TO ADJUST YOUR BUDGET

### **INCREASE BUDGET IF:**

✅ Escalating dose (GLP-1s increase over time)
✅ Adding new peptide (expanding protocol)
✅ Vendor price increases
✅ Switching to higher-dose peptides (Tirz/Reta)

### **DECREASE BUDGET IF:**

✅ Finishing recovery protocol (BPC/TB-500 are temporary)
✅ Reaching maintenance dose (no more escalation)
✅ Finding cheaper vendors
✅ Reducing waste (tracking better)
