## THE PANIC

**Friday, 8PM:**

You go to grab your Semaglutide for Sunday's dose.

You open the fridge.

**The vial is nearly empty.**

*"Oh shit. When was the last time I ordered?"*

You check your usual vendor: **Out of stock.**

You check 3 backup vendors: **Out of stock, out of stock, backordered.**

**THE PANIC SETS IN:**

*"My protocol is going to get interrupted!"*
*"Should I just order from anyone who has it?"*
*"Do I pay $40 for overnight shipping?"*

You frantically order from a sketchy vendor you've never used. You pay 2x normal price. You pay $35 for rushed shipping.

**Total panic-buy cost: $150 (should've been $50).**

**And this isn't the first time.**

**Here's how to never panic-buy peptides again.**

---

## 🚨 THE PANIC-BUYING TRIGGERS

### **TRIGGER #1: NOT TRACKING REMAINING DOSES**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You don't know how many doses are left in your current vial.

*"Is this vial half full? Quarter full? Two doses left?"*

**You realize you're out when it's TOO LATE.**

**Result:** Scramble to order, pay rush shipping, interrupt protocol.

---

### **TRIGGER #2: NO REORDER SYSTEM**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You don't have a reorder trigger.

You think: *"I'll just order when I'm running low."*

But "running low" arrives on a Friday night when vendors are closed or out of stock.

**Result:** Protocol interrupted for 7-10 days (or more).

---

### **TRIGGER #3: VENDOR STOCKOUTS**

**THE PROBLEM:**

Your go-to vendor is out of stock.

You didn't have backup vendors researched.

**You're now scrambling to find ANY source.**

**Result:** Overpay, buy from sketchy vendor, or interrupt protocol.

---

### **TRIGGER #4: FORGETTING SHIPPING TIME**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You order with 3 days of peptide left.

Standard shipping: 7-10 days.

**Oops. You'll be out for 4-7 days.**

**Result:** Pay $35 for overnight shipping (could've avoided with planning).

---

## 📊 THE INVENTORY TRACKING SYSTEM

### **WHAT TO TRACK:**

**FOR EVERY PEPTIDE:**

1. **CURRENT INVENTORY**
   - Vials on hand (reconstituted)
   - Vials on hand (unopened/lyophilized)
   - Doses remaining in current vial

2. **USAGE RATE**
   - Doses per week
   - How long will current supply last?

3. **REORDER TRIGGER**
   - When do I need to order? (2-week supply threshold)

4. **VENDOR INFO**
   - Primary vendor (name, link, typical ship time)
   - Backup vendors (2-3 alternatives)

5. **EXPIRATION DATES**
   - Reconstituted vials (30 days)
   - Unopened vials (12-24 months)

---

### **INVENTORY TRACKING TEMPLATE:**

```
PEPTIDE: [Semaglutide]

CURRENT STOCK:
- Reconstituted: [1 vial, 2 doses left]
- Unopened: [1 vial (10mg)]

USAGE RATE:
- 1 dose per week (0.5mg/dose)
- Current supply lasts: 3 weeks

REORDER TRIGGER:
- ⚠️ Reorder when: 2 weeks supply left
- Status: REORDER NOW (only 3 weeks left)

VENDOR INFO:
- Primary: [Vendor A, 5-7 day shipping]
- Backup: [Vendor B, 3-5 day shipping]
- Last order: [1/10/26]
- Cost per mg: [$5.50/mg]

EXPIRATION:
- Active vial: Expires 2/14/26
- Unopened: Good until 10/2026

ACTION NEEDED:
□ Order 1 vial this week (to arrive before running out)
```

## ⏰ THE REORDER TRIGGER SYSTEM

### **RULE: REORDER AT 2-WEEK SUPPLY THRESHOLD**

**WHY 2 WEEKS?**

- ✅ Covers standard shipping (5-7 days)
- ✅ Buffer for delays (vendor stockouts, holidays)
- ✅ No rush shipping needed (save $30-40)
- ✅ Never interrupt protocol

---

### **HOW TO CALCULATE YOUR TRIGGER:**

**STEP 1:** Calculate weekly usage

**EXAMPLE:**
- Semaglutide: 0.5mg per week
- 10mg vial = 20 doses of 0.5mg
- 20 doses ÷ 1 dose/week = 20 weeks per vial

**STEP 2:** Identify 2-week threshold

- 20 weeks total supply
- **TRIGGER: Reorder at 2 weeks remaining**
- That's Week 18 of using the vial

**STEP 3:** Set reminder

- Set calendar reminder for Week 18
- Or track doses remaining (reorder at 2 doses left)

---

### **REORDER TRIGGER BY PEPTIDE TYPE:**

| Peptide Type | Usage Frequency | Reorder Trigger |
|--------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Weekly (GLP-1s)** | 1x/week | 2 weeks (2 doses left) |
| **Twice Daily (BPC-157)** | 14x/week | 1 week (14 doses left) |
| **Before Bed (CJC/Ipa)** | 5x/week | 10 days (7-10 doses left) |

## 🛒 THE BACKUP VENDOR STRATEGY

### **WHY YOU NEED BACKUPS:**

**PRIMARY VENDOR ISSUES:**
- Out of stock (happens often)
- Slow to restock
- Shipping delays
- Price increases
- Quality declines

**WITHOUT BACKUPS:**

You're stuck. You panic-buy from whoever has stock.

**WITH BACKUPS:**

You calmly check Vendor B or C. Order normally. No panic.

---

### **HOW TO BUILD YOUR BACKUP LIST:**

**FOR EACH PEPTIDE, IDENTIFY:**

**PRIMARY VENDOR:**
- Best cost per mg
- Fast shipping
- Reliable stock

**BACKUP VENDOR #1:**
- Good cost per mg (maybe slightly more expensive)
- Fast shipping (even faster if possible)
- Good stock availability

**BACKUP VENDOR #2:**
- Acceptable cost per mg
- Emergency source (if both others are out)

---

### **VENDOR TRACKING TEMPLATE:**

```
PEPTIDE: Semaglutide 10mg

PRIMARY: Vendor A
- Cost: $55/vial ($5.50/mg)
- Shipping: 5-7 days
- Stock: Usually available
- Last order: 1/10/26
- Notes: Best price, reliable

BACKUP #1: Vendor B
- Cost: $60/vial ($6/mg)
- Shipping: 3-5 days (faster!)
- Stock: Always in stock
- Last order: Never (backup only)
- Notes: More expensive but reliable backup

BACKUP #2: Vendor C
- Cost: $70/vial ($7/mg)
- Shipping: 2-3 days
- Stock: Usually available
- Last order: Never (emergency only)
- Notes: Most expensive, but fast emergency option

DECISION TREE:
1. Try Primary (best price)
2. If out of stock → Try Backup #1 (good price, fast)
3. If both out → Use Backup #2 (expensive but fast)
```

## 💡 THE BULK ORDERING STRATEGY (ADVANCED)

### **WHEN TO BULK ORDER:**

**GOOD REASONS:**
- ✅ Vendor discount for 3+ vials (10-20% off)
- ✅ Reduce shipping costs (order less frequently)
- ✅ You've used this peptide for 6+ months (proven protocol)
- ✅ Peptide is frequently out of stock (secure supply)

**BAD REASONS:**
- ❌ First time trying this peptide (might not tolerate it)
- ❌ No storage space (will expire before using)
- ❌ Vendor you've never used (untested quality)

---

### **BULK ORDER SAFETY RULES:**

**RULE #1:** Only bulk order peptides you've used for 3+ months

**RULE #2:** Only order what you'll use in 6-12 months
- Lyophilized peptides: 12-24 month shelf life (freezer)
- Don't order more than you can use

**RULE #3:** Only bulk from vetted vendors
- You've ordered from them before
- COAs available
- Consistent quality

**RULE #4:** Factor in expiration
- Reconstituted = 30 days max
- Don't reconstitute more than you'll use in 30 days

## 🚨 WHAT TO DO WHEN YOU'RE ALREADY OUT

### **EMERGENCY PROTOCOL:**

**STEP 1: DON'T PANIC**

Missing 1-2 doses won't ruin your protocol. It's okay.

---

**STEP 2: CHECK ALL VENDORS**

- Primary vendor
- Backup vendors
- Forums/communities (who has stock?)

---

**STEP 3: EVALUATE SHIPPING OPTIONS**

**IF VENDOR HAS STOCK:**

- Standard shipping (5-7 days): Miss 1-2 doses
- Expedited shipping (2-3 days): Miss 0-1 doses
- Overnight ($35+ extra): Get tomorrow

**DECISION:**
- **GLP-1s (weekly):** Standard shipping probably fine (miss 1 dose)
- **Daily peptides:** Consider expedited (reduce protocol interruption)

---

**STEP 4: ADJUST PROTOCOL IF NEEDED**

**IF DELAY IS UNAVOIDABLE:**

- Note the gap in your tracking (for future reference)
- Resume protocol when peptides arrive
- Don't try to "make up" missed doses (just continue normally)

**IT'S NOT IDEAL, BUT IT'S NOT A DISASTER.**
