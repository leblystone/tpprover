## What You Actually Need to Track

Skip the 47-column spreadsheet. Here's what matters:

### The Non-Negotiables:
1. **Date & Time** - Not "morning," actual time
2. **What & How Much** - Full peptide name + exact dose
3. **Where You Injected** - Rotation prevents lumps
4. **Reconstitution Date** - When you mixed it + expiration (usually 30 days)
5. **How You Felt** - Side effects, energy, appetite changes

That's it. Everything else is optional.

## GLP-1 Tracking (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, etc.)

These are special because of dose escalation:

**Week 1-4:** 0.25mg  
**Week 5-8:** 0.5mg  
**Week 9-12:** 1.0mg  
*(Your protocol may vary)*

### Track This:
- Which week you're on
- Current target dose
- How you tolerated it
- Side effects (usually peak Day 2-3 post-injection)
- When to escalate next

**The Key Question:** Are you building tolerance or getting wrecked? This determines if you escalate or stay put.

## The Mistakes That Cost You Money

### 1. Not Labeling Vials
Mystery vials = wasted money. Label IMMEDIATELY when reconstituting:
- Peptide name
- Concentration
- Recon date
- Expiration

### 2. Forgetting Reconstitution Dates
Most peptides = 30 days refrigerated after mixing. Set calendar reminders or accept that you'll waste $50 injecting dead peptide.

### 3. Vague Observations
❌ "Felt off today"  
✅ "Moderate nausea 2-3 hours post-injection, resolved by evening"

Pattern recognition needs specifics.

### 4. Not Rotating Injection Sites
Same spot repeatedly = lumps and bruising. Track where you injected last time. Rotate systematically.

## The Lazy Person's Minimum System

Not tracking everything? At MINIMUM track these four:

1. Date + Time of injection
2. Peptide + Dose
3. Reconstitution date (label the vial!)
4. Major side effects

Perfect? No.  
Infinitely better than nothing? Yes.
