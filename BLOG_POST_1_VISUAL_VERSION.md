## ✅ What You Actually Need to Track

Skip the 47-column spreadsheet. Here's what matters:

### The 5 Non-Negotiables:

**📅 Date & Time**  
Not "morning"—actual time

**💊 What & How Much**  
Full peptide name + exact dose

**📍 Where You Injected**  
Rotation prevents lumps

**🧪 Reconstitution Date**  
When you mixed it + expiration (usually 30 days)

**📝 How You Felt**  
Side effects, energy, appetite changes

---

> **💡 Pro Tip:** If tracking takes longer than actually injecting, you won't do it. Keep it simple.

## 💉 GLP-1 Tracking (Sema, Tirz, Reta)

These are special because of dose escalation:

| Week | Dose |
|------|------|
| 1-4 | 0.25mg |
| 5-8 | 0.5mg |
| 9-12 | 1.0mg |

*(Your protocol may vary)*

### Track This for GLP-1s:

🔸 **Which week** you're on  
🔸 **Current target** dose  
🔸 **How you tolerated** it  
🔸 **Side effects** (usually peak Day 2-3)  
🔸 **When to escalate** next  

---

> **🎯 The Key Question:** Are you building tolerance or getting wrecked? This determines if you escalate or stay put.

## 💸 The Mistakes That Cost You Money

### ❌ Mistake #1: Not Labeling Vials

Mystery vials = wasted money.

**Label IMMEDIATELY:**
- Peptide name
- Concentration
- Recon date
- Expiration

**[Image: Example of properly labeled peptide vial]**

---

### ❌ Mistake #2: Forgetting Recon Dates

Most peptides = **30 days refrigerated** after mixing.

Set calendar reminders or accept wasting $50 on dead peptide.

---

### ❌ Mistake #3: Vague Observations

**Don't write this:**  
❌ *"Felt off today"*

**Write this instead:**  
✅ *"Moderate nausea 2-3 hours post-injection, resolved by evening"*

Pattern recognition needs specifics.

---

### ❌ Mistake #4: Not Rotating Sites

Same spot repeatedly = lumps and bruising.

**Track injection sites:**
- Abdomen (4 quadrants)
- Thighs (left/right)
- Upper arms (if IM)

Rotate systematically. Give each site at least a week to recover.

## 🎯 The Lazy Person's Minimum System

Not tracking everything? At MINIMUM track these **4 things:**

1. 📅 **Date + Time** of injection
2. 💊 **Peptide + Dose**
3. 🧪 **Reconstitution date** (label the vial!)
4. 📝 **Major side effects**

Perfect? No.  
Infinitely better than nothing? **Yes.**
