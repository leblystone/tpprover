Let's be real: we've all played "guess which vial this is" at some point.

You order from 3 different vendors because one has BPC on sale, another has better Semaglutide pricing, and the third just restocked Tirzepatide.

**Three weeks later:**

You're staring at unlabeled vials thinking *"Was this the expensive one or the backup?"*

**OR WORSE:**

*"Is this even still good? When did I order this?"* 🤔

If you've ever wasted peptides because you forgot what you had, welcome to the club. Here's how to fix it.

---

## 🎯 WHAT YOU ACTUALLY NEED TO TRACK

Skip the complicated inventory systems. Here's what matters:

### **THE 5 MUST-TRACK DETAILS:**

1. 📦 **VENDOR NAME**
   - Pricing varies wildly between vendors
   - Track who has the best deals

2. 💰 **WHAT YOU PAID**
   - Know your cost per mg
   - Catch price increases early

3. 📅 **ORDER DATE**
   - Lyophilized peptides have shelf life
   - Know what to use first

4. 🧪 **WHAT YOU HAVE**
   - How many vials of each peptide
   - Prevents panic reordering

5. ❄️ **STORAGE LOCATION**
   - Main fridge vs backup freezer
   - Reconstituted vs lyophilized

## 💡 THE VENDOR COMPARISON TRICK

Want to save serious money? Track this:

### **COST PER MG FORMULA:**

```
Total Cost ÷ Total mg = Cost per mg
```

**EXAMPLE:**

**Vendor A:** 10mg vial for $45 = **$4.50/mg**
**Vendor B:** 5mg vial for $20 = **$4.00/mg**

Vendor B is cheaper per mg, even though the vial costs less upfront.

**💰 MOST RESEARCHERS WASTE 15-20% BY NOT TRACKING THIS.**

## 🚨 THE MISTAKES THAT WASTE MONEY

### **MISTAKE #1: NO VENDOR NOTES**

**THE PROBLEM:** You forget which vendor shipped fast vs which took 3 weeks

**THE FIX:** Add vendor notes with:
- Ship time
- Packaging quality  
- Customer service rating
- Restock patterns

---

### **MISTAKE #2: NOT TRACKING STORAGE LOCATION**

**THE PROBLEM:** "I KNOW I have BPC-157... somewhere..."

**THE FIX:** Label storage zones:
- 📍 Main fridge (active peptides)
- 📍 Freezer (backup stock)
- 📍 Reconstituted (use within 30 days)

---

### **MISTAKE #3: ORDERING TOO LATE**

**THE PROBLEM:** You run out mid-protocol and have to wait 2 weeks for shipping

**THE FIX:** Set reorder triggers:
- 2 weeks supply remaining = time to reorder
- Track average shipping time per vendor
- Keep 1 backup vial for critical peptides
