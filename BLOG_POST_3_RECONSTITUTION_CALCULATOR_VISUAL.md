## 🧮 THE RECONSTITUTION FORMULA

Don't panic - this is simpler than it looks:

### **THE BASIC FORMULA:**

```
(Peptide mg × Your Dose mg) ÷ Total mL = Injection Volume (mL)
```

**EXAMPLE:**

You have:
- 10mg peptide vial
- Want 0.5mg per dose
- Mixed with 2mL bacteriostatic water

**Calculation:**
```
(10mg × 0.5mg) ÷ 2mL = 0.1mL per injection
```

**☝️ You need to inject 0.1mL (or 10 units on an insulin syringe).**

## ⚠️ THE 3 RECONSTITUTION MISTAKES

### **MISTAKE #1: FORGETTING WHAT YOU MIXED**

**THE PROBLEM:**

*"Did I use 1mL or 2mL of BAC water? I don't remember..."*

Now your entire calculation is wrong.

**THE FIX:**

🏷️ **LABEL THE VIAL IMMEDIATELY:**
- Peptide name
- Total mg
- BAC water amount
- Date mixed
- Dose per 0.1mL (for quick reference)

**EXAMPLE LABEL:**
```
Sema 5mg
+ 2mL BAC
Mixed: 1/15/26
0.1mL = 0.25mg
Exp: 2/14/26
```

---

### **MISTAKE #2: NOT CONVERTING MG TO MCG**

**THE PROBLEM:**

Some peptides dose in **mg** (milligrams), others in **mcg** (micrograms).

**1mg = 1000mcg**

**THE FIX:**

Always convert to the same unit before calculating:

**EXAMPLE - BPC-157:**
- Typical dose: 250mcg (0.25mg)
- NOT 250mg (that would be 1000x overdose!)

---

### **MISTAKE #3: USING THE WRONG SYRINGE SCALE**

**THE PROBLEM:**

Insulin syringes show "units" not mL.

**THE CONVERSION:**
- **0.1mL = 10 units**
- **0.05mL = 5 units**
- **0.025mL = 2.5 units**

**THE FIX:**

Use this quick conversion:

**0.1mL = 10 units = "10 on the syringe"**

## 🧪 STEP-BY-STEP RECONSTITUTION CHECKLIST

**BEFORE YOU START:**

□ Gather peptide vial, BAC water, alcohol wipes, syringe

□ Let peptide reach room temp (if refrigerated)

□ Calculate your reconstitution ratio

□ Prepare vial label

---

**DURING RECONSTITUTION:**

□ Clean rubber stopper with alcohol wipe

□ Draw exact amount of BAC water (measure carefully)

□ Inject water slowly down the side of the vial (NOT directly on powder)

□ Swirl gently (never shake) until dissolved

□ Apply label with all info (peptide, mg, BAC amount, date, dose)

---

**AFTER RECONSTITUTION:**

□ Store in refrigerator immediately

□ Log in tracking system (app or planner)

□ Set reminder for 30-day expiration

□ Calculate dose volume before first injection
