## THE MEMORY PROBLEM

**Week 1:** *"I feel amazing! This peptide is incredible!"*

**THE REALIZATION:**

Without data, you're just guessing.

You don't know if:
- The peptide is working
- Side effects are getting worse
- You should escalate dose
- You're wasting money

**This is why serious researchers keep journals.**

Here's what to track (and what to skip).

---

## 🎯 WHAT TO ACTUALLY TRACK

Skip the 47-column spreadsheet. Here's what matters:

### **THE 5 ESSENTIAL DATA POINTS:**

1. 📊 **HOW YOU FEEL** (energy, mood, focus)
   - Rate 1-10 daily
   - Note patterns over time

2. ⚖️ **BODY METRICS** (weight, measurements if relevant)
   - Weekly weigh-ins (same day/time)
   - Monthly measurements

3. 💪 **SIDE EFFECTS** (good and bad)
   - When they started
   - When they resolved
   - Severity (mild/moderate/severe)

4. 🧪 **WHAT YOU'RE TAKING** (dose, time, rotation site)
   - Link to dosage log
   - Track any dose changes

5. 🔄 **PROTOCOL CHANGES** (dose escalation, additions, breaks)
   - Document why you changed
   - Track results after change

## 💡 THE SIDE EFFECT TRACKING TRICK

Most people track side effects wrong. Here's the right way:

### **DON'T JUST NOTE "NAUSEA"**

**TRACK THIS INSTEAD:**

| Date | Time | Severity (1-10) | Duration | What Helped | Notes |
|------|------|-----------------|----------|-------------|-------|
| 1/15 | 2PM  | 6/10           | 2 hours  | Ginger tea  | Peaked 30min post-injection |
| 1/22 | 2PM  | 4/10           | 1 hour   | Ginger tea  | Less severe - building tolerance? |
| 1/29 | 2PM  | 2/10           | 30min    | Nothing     | Almost gone |

**WHY THIS MATTERS:**

✅ You can see tolerance building
✅ You know what helps
✅ You can communicate clearly with providers
✅ You know if you should push through or stop

## 🚨 THE MISTAKES THAT WASTE TIME

### **MISTAKE #1: ONLY TRACKING WHEN THINGS GO WRONG**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You only write when you feel terrible. When you feel good, you skip it.

**Result:** Your journal looks like a horror story.

**THE FIX:**

📅 **Track EVERY day** (even "boring" normal days)

**WHY:** You need baseline data to compare against.

---

### **MISTAKE #2: VAGUE OBSERVATIONS**

**DON'T WRITE THIS:**

*"Felt off today"*

**WRITE THIS INSTEAD:**

*"Energy 4/10 (usual 7/10). Mild nausea 2 hours post-injection. Resolved by dinner. Same dose as last week."*

**WHY:** Specific data lets you spot patterns.

---

### **MISTAKE #3: NOT TRACKING PROTOCOL CONTEXT**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You log "felt amazing today!" but forget to note:
- Did you increase dose?
- Did you change injection time?
- Did you add a new peptide?

**THE FIX:**

🔗 **Link journal entries to your dosage log**

Always note what changed in your protocol.
