## 🎯 WHAT TO ACTUALLY COMPARE

Price is important. But it's not everything.

### **THE 7 VENDOR COMPARISON FACTORS:**

1. 💰 **COST PER MG** (not just "price per vial")
2. 📦 **SHIPPING SPEED & RELIABILITY**
3. 🧪 **TESTING & COAs** (third-party verification)
4. 📱 **CUSTOMER SERVICE** (do they respond?)
5. ⭐ **PACKAGING QUALITY** (cold shipping, secure packaging)
6. 🔄 **RESTOCK RELIABILITY** (are they always "out of stock"?)
7. 💳 **PAYMENT OPTIONS** (crypto, credit, PayPal?)

---

## 💡 THE COST PER MG CALCULATOR

This is the ONLY way to compare pricing fairly.

### **THE FORMULA:**

```
Total Price ÷ Total MG = Cost per MG
```

**EXAMPLE COMPARISON:**

**VENDOR A:**
- 10mg vial for $50
- $50 ÷ 10mg = **$5.00/mg**

**VENDOR B:**
- 5mg vial for $22
- $22 ÷ 5mg = **$4.40/mg**

**VENDOR C:**
- 15mg vial for $60
- $60 ÷ 15mg = **$4.00/mg**

**WINNER: Vendor C** (even though total price is higher)

## 🚨 VENDOR RED FLAGS (AVOID THESE)

### **RED FLAG #1: TOO CHEAP TO BE TRUE**

**THE PROBLEM:**

If everyone else sells 10mg Tirz for $80-100, and someone's selling it for $30... something's wrong.

**WHAT IT USUALLY MEANS:**
- Underdosed peptides
- Fake peptides
- No quality control
- Exit scam waiting to happen

**THE FIX:**

✅ **Compare to market average pricing**
✅ **If it's 50%+ cheaper than everyone else, it's suspicious**

---

### **RED FLAG #2: NO COAs OR "TRUST US"**

**THE PROBLEM:**

*"We test everything in-house, just trust us!"*

**THE FIX:**

🧪 **Require third-party testing (Janoshik, COA available)**

If they won't provide COAs, they're hiding something.

---

### **RED FLAG #3: CONSTANTLY "OUT OF STOCK"**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You find a great vendor. Then:
- Week 1: "Out of stock"
- Week 3: "Back in stock!" (you order)
- Week 5: "Out of stock again"

**Result:** You can't run consistent protocols.

**THE FIX:**

📋 **Track restock patterns for each vendor**

Use 2-3 reliable vendors (not just one).

---

### **RED FLAG #4: TERRIBLE COMMUNICATION**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You email with a question. Crickets for 5 days.

Your order is delayed. No tracking update.

**THE FIX:**

✅ **Test customer service BEFORE placing big orders**
- Send a pre-order question
- See how fast they respond
- Check if they're helpful or dismissive

---

### **RED FLAG #5: SKETCHY PAYMENT METHODS ONLY**

**THE PROBLEM:**

*"We only accept Bitcoin sent to this random wallet, no refunds."*

**THE FIX:**

💳 **Prefer vendors with buyer protection:**
- Credit card (you can dispute charges)
- PayPal (buyer protection)
- Crypto is fine IF vendor is established with good reviews

## 💰 HOW TO SAVE MONEY WITHOUT SACRIFICING QUALITY

### **STRATEGY #1: BUY LARGER VIALS**

**EXAMPLE:**

5mg vial = $25 ($5/mg)
10mg vial = $40 ($4/mg)

**Savings:** $1/mg = $10 per vial

**ANNUAL SAVINGS:** $120+ for regular researchers

---

### **STRATEGY #2: BULK ORDERS (WITH CAUTION)**

Some vendors offer:
- 10% off 3+ vials
- 15% off 5+ vials

**✅ DO THIS IF:**
- You've used this vendor before (trusted)
- Peptide has long shelf life (lyophilized)
- You use this peptide regularly

**❌ DON'T DO THIS IF:**
- New vendor (untested)
- First time trying this peptide
- Vendor has reliability issues

---

### **STRATEGY #3: TRACK SEASONAL PRICING**

**PEPTIDE PRICING PATTERNS:**

🔴 **EXPENSIVE TIMES:**
- January (New Year's resolutions)
- Summer (beach season prep)

🟢 **CHEAPER TIMES:**
- Late spring
- Late fall

**💡 TIP:** Stock up during cheaper periods if you have storage space.

---

### **STRATEGY #4: JOIN VENDOR EMAIL LISTS**

Most vendors send:
- Restock alerts
- Sales notifications (10-20% off)
- New product launches

**Worth it for your top 2-3 vendors.**

## 🎯 THE VENDOR EVALUATION CHECKLIST

**BEFORE FIRST ORDER:**

□ Check if COAs are available for products
□ Read reviews from multiple sources
□ Test customer service (send a question)
□ Verify payment methods
□ Check shipping policy (cold shipping?)
□ Calculate cost per mg vs competitors

**AFTER FIRST ORDER:**

□ Rate packaging quality (1-10)
□ Track actual shipping time
□ Verify product quality (effectiveness)
□ Note customer service experience
□ Compare actual cost to advertised price
□ Would you order again? (Yes/No)

## THE VENDOR ROTATION STRATEGY

**DON'T PUT ALL YOUR EGGS IN ONE BASKET.**

### **THE SMART APPROACH:**

✅ **PRIMARY VENDOR:** 70% of orders
- Best balance of price/speed/quality
- Most reliable

✅ **BACKUP VENDOR:** 20% of orders
- Use occasionally to maintain relationship
- Available if primary is out of stock

✅ **TESTING VENDOR:** 10% of orders
- Try new vendors with small orders
- Test quality before committing

**WHY THIS WORKS:**

- You're never stuck if one vendor fails
- You can compare quality side-by-side
- You find better deals over time
