## 🧬 WHAT ARE GLP-1 PEPTIDES?

### **THE SCIENCE (SIMPLIFIED):**

GLP-1 peptides mimic hormones that:
- ✅ Regulate appetite
- ✅ Slow gastric emptying (you feel full longer)
- ✅ Improve insulin sensitivity
- ✅ Support metabolic health

**WHY RESEARCHERS USE THEM:**

Primary research areas:
- Weight management research
- Metabolic health studies
- Appetite regulation studies
- Insulin sensitivity research

## 💊 SEMAGLUTIDE (SEMA) - THE STARTER

### **WHAT IT IS:**

- GLP-1 receptor agonist
- Most researched GLP-1 peptide
- Brand names: Ozempic, Wegovy

---

### **TYPICAL RESEARCH PROTOCOL:**

**ESCALATION SCHEDULE:**

| Week | Dose | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| 1-4 | 0.25mg | Starting dose (assess tolerance) |
| 5-8 | 0.5mg | First increase |
| 9-12 | 1mg | Moderate dose |
| 13-16 | 1.7mg | Higher dose |
| 17+ | 2.4mg | Max dose (if needed) |

**⏱️ Inject:** Once weekly (same day each week)

**⚠️ Common Side Effects:**
- Nausea (especially weeks 1-4)
- Reduced appetite
- Fatigue
- Constipation
- Potential gallbladder concerns (long-term)

---

### **SEMA PROS:**

✅ Most affordable GLP-1
✅ Most available (easy to source)
✅ Extensive research history
✅ Well-understood side effect profile
✅ Good for beginners

---

### **SEMA CONS:**

❌ Slower results than Tirz/Reta
❌ Only GLP-1 pathway (not dual/triple)
❌ May plateau at higher doses

---

### **BEST FOR:**

- First-time GLP-1 researchers
- Budget-conscious research
- Established safety profile needed

---

## 💊 TIRZEPATIDE (TIRZ) - THE UPGRADE

### **WHAT IT IS:**

- **Dual agonist**: GLP-1 + GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide)
- More pathways = potentially better results
- Brand name: Mounjaro, Zepbound

---

### **TYPICAL RESEARCH PROTOCOL:**

**ESCALATION SCHEDULE:**

| Week | Dose | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| 1-4 | 2.5mg | Starting dose |
| 5-8 | 5mg | First increase |
| 9-12 | 7.5mg | Moderate dose |
| 13-16 | 10mg | Higher dose |
| 17-20 | 12.5mg | Very high dose |
| 21+ | 15mg | Max dose (if needed) |

**⏱️ Inject:** Once weekly (same day each week)

**⚠️ Common Side Effects:**
- Nausea (often stronger than Sema)
- Significant appetite suppression
- GI issues (diarrhea, constipation)
- Fatigue
- Hair thinning (some researchers)

---

### **TIRZ PROS:**

✅ Dual-action (GLP-1 + GIP)
✅ Often better results than Sema alone
✅ Well-studied (good safety data)
✅ Widely available

---

### **TIRZ CONS:**

❌ More expensive than Sema
❌ Stronger side effects for some
❌ Higher doses needed (more costly)

---

### **BEST FOR:**

- Researchers who didn't get results with Sema
- Those willing to pay more for dual-action
- Researchers comfortable with stronger effects

---

## 💊 RETATRUTIDE (RETA) - THE NEWEST

### **WHAT IT IS:**

- **Triple agonist**: GLP-1 + GIP + Glucagon
- Newest GLP-1 peptide (less long-term research)
- No brand name yet (still in trials)

---

### **TYPICAL RESEARCH PROTOCOL:**

**ESCALATION SCHEDULE (COMMON):**

| Week | Dose | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| 1-4 | 2mg | Starting dose (assess tolerance) |
| 5-8 | 4mg | First increase |
| 9-12 | 6mg | Moderate dose |
| 13-16 | 8mg | Higher dose |
| 17+ | 12mg | Max dose (research varies) |

**⏱️ Inject:** Once weekly (same day each week)

**⚠️ Common Side Effects:**
- Strongest nausea potential
- Significant appetite suppression
- GI distress (more common)
- Increased heart rate (some researchers)
- Less long-term safety data

---

### **RETA PROS:**

✅ Triple-action (most pathways targeted)
✅ Potentially strongest results
✅ Cutting-edge research

---

### **RETA CONS:**

❌ Most expensive
❌ Less available (harder to source)
❌ Least amount of long-term data
❌ Strongest side effects
❌ Less predictable responses

---

### **BEST FOR:**

- Experienced GLP-1 researchers
- Those who've plateaued on Sema/Tirz
- Willing to accept less safety data
- Budget not a concern

## 📝 SIDE EFFECT MANAGEMENT TIPS

### **FOR NAUSEA (ALL GLP-1s):**

✅ **WHAT HELPS:**
- Ginger tea or ginger supplements
- Eat smaller, more frequent meals
- Avoid high-fat foods (slows digestion more)
- Stay hydrated
- Inject before bed (sleep through worst)

---

### **FOR CONSTIPATION:**

✅ **WHAT HELPS:**
- Increase water intake
- Add fiber (psyllium husk)
- Magnesium supplements
- Light exercise

---

### **FOR APPETITE SUPPRESSION:**

✅ **WHAT HELPS:**
- Focus on protein (prevent muscle loss)
- Eat even when not hungry (small portions)
- Track calories (avoid going too low)
- Consider protein shakes

## 🔬 SHOULD YOU COMBINE GLP-1s?

**SHORT ANSWER: NO.**

❌ **Don't stack:**
- Sema + Tirz
- Tirz + Reta
- Any GLP-1 + another GLP-1

**WHY:**
- Duplicate pathways
- Increased side effects
- No additional benefit
- More expensive

**✅ OKAY TO COMBINE:**
- GLP-1 + BPC-157 (recovery support)
- GLP-1 + CJC/Ipamorelin (muscle preservation)
