## 🚨 MISTAKE #1: NOT TRACKING DOSES

## 🚨 MISTAKE #2: WRONG RECONSTITUTION MATH

### **THE PROBLEM:**

*"I'll just eyeball the dose... 0.1mL is probably close enough."*

You mess up the math. You're injecting:
- Too much (run out early, waste money)
- Too little (no results, waste money)
- Wrong concentration entirely

---

### **THE COST:**

**SCENARIO:**

You have a 10mg vial of Tirzepatide ($100).

You reconstitute with 2mL BAC water.

**CORRECT DOSE:** 0.5mL = 2.5mg

**YOUR MISTAKE:** You inject 0.75mL thinking it's 2.5mg

**ACTUAL DOSE:** 3.75mg (50% overdose!)

**Result:** Your $100 vial lasts 2.5 weeks instead of 4 weeks.

**💸 WASTED: $40 per vial**

---

### **THE FIX:**

✅ **ALWAYS USE A CALCULATOR:**

**FORMULA:**
```
(Total mg ÷ Total mL) × Desired dose = Injection volume
```

**TOOLS:**
- [Built-in calculator in The Pep Planner app →](https://thepepplanner.com/shop/p/monthly-pep-planner-app)
- Online peptide calculators (bookmark one)
- [Read our reconstitution guide →](#)

**💰 MONEY SAVED:** $100-300/year

## 🚨 MISTAKE #3: STORING IN THE FRIDGE DOOR

### **THE PROBLEM:**

You store peptides in the fridge door (most convenient spot).

**Fridge door = warmest, most unstable temperature zone**

Every time you open the fridge:
- Temperature rises 5-10°F
- Peptides degrade slightly

Over weeks/months = significant degradation = reduced effectiveness.

---

### **THE FIX:**

✅ **STORE IN THE BACK OF THE FRIDGE:**
- Most consistent temperature
- Coldest spot (without freezing)
- Least affected by door opening

**💰 MONEY SAVED:** $50-100/year (from degraded peptides)

## 🚨 MISTAKE #4: NOT LABELING RECONSTITUTION DATES

### **THE PROBLEM:**

*"When did I mix this vial? 3 weeks ago? 5 weeks ago?"*

**Most reconstituted peptides expire in 30 days.**

You're probably using expired, less effective peptides.

---

### **THE FIX:**

✅ **LABEL EVERY VIAL IMMEDIATELY:**

**WRITE ON VIAL:**
```
[PEPTIDE NAME]
Mixed: [DATE]
⚠️ EXPIRES: [30 DAYS LATER]
```

**SET REMINDERS:**
- Phone calendar (7 days before expiration)
- [Auto-expiration tracking in The Pep Planner app](https://thepepplanner.com/shop/p/monthly-pep-planner-app)

**💰 MONEY SAVED:** $100-150/year (wasted expired vials)

## 🚨 MISTAKE #5: BUYING BASED ON PRICE ALONE

### **THE PROBLEM:**

You see: *"5mg Semaglutide - Only $20!"*

You think: *"That's half the price of my usual vendor!"*

You order 5 vials. **$100 total.**

**THEN:**
- Takes 3 weeks to ship
- Vials aren't labeled
- No COA
- Peptide doesn't work (underdosed or fake)

**You wasted $100 chasing a "deal."**

---

### **THE FIX:**

✅ **COMPARE COST PER MG, NOT JUST PRICE:**

**FORMULA:**
```
Total Price ÷ Total MG = Cost per MG
```

**EXAMPLE:**

Vendor A: 5mg for $20 = $4/mg
Vendor B: 10mg for $50 = $5/mg

**Vendor A looks cheaper... but:**
- No COA = risky
- Slow shipping = inconsistent supply
- Bad packaging = potential degradation

**Vendor B costs more per vial but:**
- Always has COAs
- Ships in 2 days
- Cold packs included
- Reliable

**✅ BEST CHOICE:** Vendor B (pay slightly more for reliability)

**💰 MONEY SAVED:** $200-400/year (avoiding fake/underdosed peptides)

## 🚨 MISTAKE #7: ESCALATING DOSE TOO FAST

### **THE PROBLEM:**

**Week 1:** Start Semaglutide at 0.25mg
**Week 2:** *"I don't feel anything, let me jump to 1mg!"*

**Result:**
- Overwhelming side effects (nausea, fatigue)
- Can't tolerate it
- Have to drop back down or quit

**You wasted money rushing.**

---

### **THE FIX:**

✅ **FOLLOW STANDARD ESCALATION PROTOCOLS:**

**SEMAGLUTIDE EXAMPLE:**
- Weeks 1-4: 0.25mg (build tolerance)
- Weeks 5-8: 0.5mg (first increase)
- Weeks 9-12: 1mg (continue)

**WHY IT MATTERS:**
- Builds tolerance gradually
- Minimizes side effects
- Better long-term adherence
- Consistent results

**💰 MONEY SAVED:** $100-200/year (from abandoned protocols)

## 🚨 MISTAKE #8: USING THE SAME INJECTION SITE

### **THE PROBLEM:**

You inject in the same spot every time (usually belly, right side).

**Result:**
- Scar tissue builds up
- Reduced absorption
- Painful injections
- Less effective peptides

---

### **THE FIX:**

✅ **ROTATE INJECTION SITES:**

**COMMON SITES:**
- Abdomen (avoid 2" around navel)
- Thighs (outer/front)
- Upper arms (back/outer)

**ROTATION STRATEGY:**
- Divide each area into quadrants
- Rotate through quadrants
- Don't reuse same spot for 2 weeks

**TRACK ROTATION:**
- [Auto-rotation tracking in The Pep Planner app →](https://thepepplanner.com/shop/p/monthly-pep-planner-app)
- Mark on body diagram in physical planner

**💰 VALUE:** Better absorption = better results = don't waste peptides
