## 🧬 WHAT ARE RECOVERY PEPTIDES?

### **BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound):**

- Synthetic peptide derived from stomach protein
- Promotes healing of muscles, tendons, ligaments
- Supports gut health (bonus)
- Reduces inflammation

**RESEARCH AREAS:**
- Tendon/ligament injuries
- Muscle tears
- Joint pain
- Gut inflammation
- Wound healing

---

### **TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4):**

- Naturally occurring peptide in the body
- Promotes cell migration and differentiation
- Supports tissue repair
- Reduces inflammation

**RESEARCH AREAS:**
- Muscle recovery
- Tendon/ligament healing
- Flexibility/range of motion
- Chronic injuries
- Post-surgery healing

## 💊 BPC-157 DOSING PROTOCOLS

### **TYPICAL RESEARCH PROTOCOL:**

**STANDARD DOSE:** 250-500mcg per injection

**FREQUENCY:** Once or twice daily (every 12 hours if twice)

**DURATION:** 4-6 weeks (or until healed)

---

### **DOSING BY INJURY TYPE:**

**ACUTE INJURY (recent):**
- 250mcg twice daily (morning + evening)
- Duration: 2-4 weeks
- Inject near injury site

**CHRONIC INJURY (long-term):**
- 500mcg twice daily
- Duration: 4-8 weeks
- Inject near injury site

**GUT HEALTH:**
- 250-500mcg once daily
- Duration: 4-6 weeks
- Subcutaneous injection (anywhere)

---

### **INJECTION SITES FOR BPC-157:**

**OPTION 1: LOCAL INJECTION (PREFERRED)**
- Inject within 2-3 inches of injury site
- Intramuscular or subcutaneous
- Example: Shoulder injury → inject near deltoid

**OPTION 2: SYSTEMIC INJECTION**
- Standard subcutaneous (abdomen, thigh)
- Still effective, just slower
- Better for gut health or multiple injuries

## 🚨 COMMON RECOVERY PEPTIDE MISTAKES

### **MISTAKE #1: GIVING UP TOO EARLY**

**THE PROBLEM:**

*"I've been using BPC-157 for 5 days and nothing's changed!"*

**Recovery takes time.** Most researchers see results in 2-4 weeks.

**THE FIX:**

✅ Commit to at least 4 weeks
✅ Track daily progress (small wins add up)
✅ Be patient

---

### **MISTAKE #2: NOT INJECTING NEAR INJURY SITE (BPC-157)**

**THE PROBLEM:**

You inject BPC-157 in your abdomen for a shoulder injury.

**BPC-157 works best locally** (within 2-3 inches of injury).

**THE FIX:**

✅ Inject as close to injury as safely possible
✅ Ask a healthcare provider if unsure about injection sites

---

### **MISTAKE #3: SKIPPING DOSES**

**THE PROBLEM:**

Inconsistent dosing = inconsistent healing.

**THE FIX:**

✅ Set daily reminders (for BPC-157)
✅ Schedule weekly TB-500 injections
✅ [Use The Pep Planner app for dose tracking →](https://thepepplanner.com/shop/p/monthly-pep-planner-app)

---

### **MISTAKE #4: NOT RESTING THE INJURY**

**THE PROBLEM:**

*"I'm using peptides, so I can push through the pain, right?"*

**WRONG.** Peptides help healing, but you still need:
- Rest (reduce stress on injured area)
- Physical therapy (proper movement patterns)
- Progressive loading (don't rush back to 100%)

**THE FIX:**

✅ Peptides + rest + PT = optimal healing
✅ Listen to your body
✅ Don't re-injure
