# Google Play Lifetime One-Time Purchase Setup

## Where to Create It

**Go to:** **Monetize with Play** → **Products** → **One-time products** → **Create product**

(Not in Subscriptions - it's a separate section!)

---

## Lifetime One-Time Purchase - All Values

### Basic Information:
- **Product ID:** `com.thepepplanner.app.lifetime`
  - ⚠️ Must match EXACTLY (case-sensitive)
- **Name:** `Lifetime Research Access` (or your preferred name)
- **Description:**
  ```
  One-time payment for lifetime access to The Pep Planner. Never pay again - get all features forever including all future updates, new features, and priority support.
  ```

### Pricing:
- **Price:** $99.99 USD (or your pricing)
- **Currency:** USD
- **Type:** One-time purchase (NOT a subscription)
- **Status:** Active

### Product Benefits (if Google Play asks for this):

**What's Included:**
- ✅ Everything Forever - All features, current and future, for life
- ✅ No Recurring Fees - One payment, lifetime access
- ✅ All Future Updates - Automatic access to all new features and improvements
- ✅ Priority Support - Lifetime priority support access
- ✅ Beta Feature Access - Early access to beta features and testing
- ✅ Grandfathered Pricing - Lock in your rate forever, even as prices increase
- ✅ Unlimited Everything - No limits on protocols, data, or features

**Perfect For:**
- Long-term researchers
- Labs with permanent research needs
- Users who want the ultimate value
- Anyone who wants to support ongoing development

### Pricing Setup:
- Set default price: **$99.99 USD**
- Enable auto-conversion for all countries
- This will set prices for Brazil, France, Italy, Spain, etc.

---

## Key Differences from Subscriptions

| Feature | Subscription | One-Time Purchase |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Location** | Products → Subscriptions | Products → One-time products |
| **Billing** | Recurring (monthly/yearly) | One-time payment |
| **Base Plan ID** | Required | Not needed |
| **Tags** | Optional | Optional |
| **Renewal** | Auto-renews | Never renews |
| **Cancellation** | Can cancel anytime | N/A (one-time) |

---

## Important Notes

1. **One-time products are separate** from subscriptions in Google Play Console
2. **No Base Plan ID needed** - that's only for subscriptions
3. **No billing period** - it's a one-time purchase
4. **No renewal** - users pay once and get access forever
5. **Same pricing setup** - use default price and auto-conversion

---

## After Creating All Three Products

Once you've created:
- ✅ Monthly subscription
- ✅ Annual subscription  
- ✅ Lifetime one-time purchase

You'll have all three products set up and ready to use in your app!
