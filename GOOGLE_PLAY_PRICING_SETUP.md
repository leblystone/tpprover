# Google Play Pricing Setup Guide

## Understanding the Error

Google Play requires you to set prices for **all countries** where your app is available. The countries showing errors need prices set.

**Good news:** You don't need to calculate VAT/taxes - Google Play handles that automatically. The percentages shown (20%, 22%, 21%) are just informational - you set the **base price** and Google adds taxes.

---

## Quick Solution: Set Default Price

### Option 1: Use Default Price (Easiest)

1. In the pricing section, look for **"Set default price"** or **"Use default price for all countries"**
2. Set your default price: **$3.99 USD** (for monthly)
3. Google Play will automatically convert this to local currencies
4. This applies to all countries, including the ones showing errors

**This is the recommended approach** - Google Play handles currency conversion automatically.

## Recommended Approach

### Use Google Play's Auto-Pricing:

1. **Set your base price in USD:** $3.99
2. **Enable "Use Google Play's suggested prices"** or **"Auto-convert prices"**
3. Google Play will:
   - Convert to local currencies
   - Adjust for purchasing power in each country
   - Handle all tax calculations
   - Keep prices consistent with your USD pricing

This is the **easiest and most reliable** method.

---

## Step-by-Step in Google Play Console

1. In the subscription pricing section, look for:
   - **"Set prices for all countries"** button, OR
   - **"Use default price"** option, OR
   - **"Auto-convert prices"** toggle

2. **Set your base price:**
   - Monthly: **$3.99 USD**
   - Annual: **$36.99 USD**
   - Lifetime: **$99.99 USD**

3. **Enable auto-conversion** (if available):
   - Toggle on "Use Google Play's suggested prices"
   - This will automatically set prices for all countries

4. **Review and save:**
   - Google Play will show you the converted prices
   - Review them (you can adjust individual countries if needed)
   - Click **Save** or **Continue**

---

## Understanding VAT/Tax Display

The percentages shown (20%, 22%, 21%) are **informational only**:
- They show what tax rate applies in that country
- **You don't need to calculate or add these** - Google Play handles it
- Users see the final price including taxes
- You receive the base price (minus Google's commission)

**Example:**
- You set price: €3.99
- User pays: €4.79 (with 20% VAT in France)
- You receive: €3.99 (minus Google's 15-30% commission)

---

## Common Pricing Strategies

### Strategy 1: Exact USD Conversion
- Set prices to match USD exactly
- Example: $3.99 USD = €3.99 EUR
- Simple but may be expensive in some countries

### Strategy 2: Purchasing Power Parity
- Let Google Play suggest prices
- Prices adjusted for local purchasing power
- Better for user acquisition in lower-income countries

### Strategy 3: Regional Pricing
- Set different prices for different regions
- More complex but can optimize revenue

**For your first setup, use Strategy 1 or 2 (auto-convert).**
