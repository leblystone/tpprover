## 📝 Google Play Console Formatting Tips

When entering in Google Play Console:

1. **Product Description** (short version) - Goes in the main description field
2. **Benefits** - Can be added in the "Subscription benefits" section if available
3. **Use bullet points** - Google Play supports formatting
4. **Keep it clear** - Focus on user value, not technical details
5. **Maintain research terminology** - Use "research," "protocols," "lab access" language

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- Keep descriptions focused on **research management** benefits
- Avoid medical or treatment language (maintain "research principal")
- Emphasize **organization, tracking, and data management**
- Highlight **value and convenience**
