## 📝 SEO Formula (Based on What Worked for Monthly Subscription)

### Structure:
```
[Primary Keyword] | [Secondary Benefit] | [Brand Name]
```

### Key Elements:
1. **Primary Keyword** (0-30 chars) - What they're searching for
2. **Secondary Benefit** (pipe separator) - Why they need it
3. **Brand Name** (short) - "Pep Planner" (NOT "— The Pep Planner")
4. **Total Length:** 60-75 characters (sweet spot for Google)

### Example (from your successful monthly subscription):

## 🔄 Managing Quarterly Design Changes

### Current Challenge:
- Your planner designs change **every quarter** (new themes, colors, aesthetics)
- Theme names like "Dune," "Midnight," "Skyline" are **temporary**

### SEO Strategy (Solves This Problem):
✅ **Use the SAME SEO title for all physical planners**, regardless of current design
✅ **Differentiate products through:**
   - Product photos (customers see the actual design)
   - Product descriptions (mention aesthetic/vibe in description)
   - Internal Squarespace product name (not the SEO title)

### Example Workflow When Designs Change:

**Q1 2026: "Dune" Design**
- **SEO Title:** "Peptide Research Planner | Track Protocols, Dosage & Stockpile | Pep Planner"
- **Product Name:** "Dune Pep Planner" (internal)
- **Description:** "Our Desert-inspired Dune design features warm, earthy tones..."

**Q2 2026: "Ocean Breeze" Design (New)**
- **SEO Title:** "Peptide Research Planner | Track Protocols, Dosage & Stockpile | Pep Planner" ← **SAME**
- **Product Name:** "Ocean Breeze Pep Planner" (internal)
- **Description:** "Our coastal-inspired Ocean Breeze design features calming blues..."

### What You DON'T Change:
- ❌ SEO Title (stays the same forever)
- ❌ Meta description keywords (stays research-focused)
- ❌ URL slug (if possible, keep consistent)

### What You DO Change:
- ✅ Product photos (show new design)
- ✅ Product description aesthetic mentions ("Desert-inspired" → "Coastal-inspired")
- ✅ Internal product name for your inventory management

### Benefits of This Approach:
1. **Zero quarterly SEO work** - Your rankings keep building
2. **Google loves consistency** - Rankings don't reset every quarter
3. **Customers find you easier** - They search "peptide planner," not "dune peptide planner"
4. **Less maintenance** - Focus on design, not SEO updates
