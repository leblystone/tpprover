## 🎯 IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (Do in Google Search Console)

### Step 1: Submit Your Sitemap (2 minutes)

1. In Google Search Console, click **Sitemaps** (left sidebar, under "Indexing")
2. In the "Add a new sitemap" field, enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Submit**
4. Wait 24-48 hours for processing

### Step 2: Request Indexing for Key Pages (10 minutes)

Go to **URL Inspection** (left sidebar) and request indexing for each:

**Priority 1 (Do First):**
- `https://thepepplanner.app/`
- `https://thepepplanner.app/faq` ⭐ **NEW - Critical for AI search!**
- `https://thepepplanner.app/features`

**Priority 2:**
- `https://thepepplanner.app/about`
- `https://thepepplanner.app/blog`
- `https://thepepplanner.app/resources`

**How to request indexing:**
1. Paste URL in URL Inspection
2. Click **Request Indexing**
3. Wait for "URL is on Google" confirmation
4. Repeat for each URL

### Step 3: Check Current Indexing Status (2 minutes)

1. Go to **Indexing > Pages** (left sidebar)
2. See which pages are currently indexed
3. Note any errors or warnings
