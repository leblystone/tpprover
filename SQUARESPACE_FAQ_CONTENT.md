### **FAQ 15: How do I use the reconstitution calculator?**

**Answer:**
```
The Pep Planner includes a reconstitution calculator reference guide. Here's how to use it:

**In the Physical Planner:**
- Find the reconstitution calculator page (included in every planner)
- Follow the formula: (Desired Dose / Vial Amount) x Reconstitution Volume
- Record your mixing date and expiration date in the tracking section
- Use the reference chart for common peptide reconstitutions

**In the App:**
- Tap "Reconstitute" in the main menu
- Enter: Vial amount (mg), Reconstitution volume (ml), Desired dose
- App automatically calculates the volume to inject
- Set expiration date reminder
- Log in your stockpile tracker

**Example:**
- 5mg vial + 2ml bacteriostatic water = 2.5mg/ml
- For 0.5mg dose: inject 0.2ml (200 units on insulin syringe)

The calculator helps prevent dosing errors and makes reconstitution simple.
```
