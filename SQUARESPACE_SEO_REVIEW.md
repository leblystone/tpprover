### 🔧 CRITICAL FIX: Remove "— The Pep Planner" Suffix

**Issue:** Squarespace auto-appends "— The Pep Planner" to ALL product SEO titles

**Evidence:**
- In The Clouds: ends with "...| Pep Planner — The Pep Planner"
- Pocket Research: ends with "...— The Pep Planner"
- Monthly subscription: ends with "...| Pep Planner — The Pep Planner"

**Impact:**
- ❌ Wastes 20 characters in every SEO title
- ❌ Makes titles too long (90+ chars instead of 70-75)
- ❌ Looks redundant and unprofessional
- ❌ Gets truncated in Google search results

**Where to Fix This:**
This is a **site-wide Squarespace setting**. You need to:

1. Go to **Settings → Marketing → SEO**
2. Look for **"Site Title"** or **"Site-wide SEO Suffix"**
3. Remove or modify the auto-append suffix

**OR**

If you want to keep the suffix for some pages, you may need to:
1. Remove "Pep Planner" from the END of your product SEO titles
2. Let Squarespace append "— The Pep Planner" automatically
3. This means your product SEO titles should end with: "...Track Protocols & Dosage"

**Recommended Product SEO (if Squarespace auto-appends):**
```
GLP-1 & Peptide Research Planner | Track Protocols & Dosage
(60 characters, becomes 80 after Squarespace adds "— The Pep Planner")
```
