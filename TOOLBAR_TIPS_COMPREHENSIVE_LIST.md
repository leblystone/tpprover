## Dashboard Tips Widget (Optional Widget)
**Location:** `src/components/dashboard/widgets/TipsWidget.jsx`  
**Rotation:** Every 20 seconds (or manual navigation)  
**Total Tips:** 35

1. **Task Syncing** 📋 - Tasks automatically sync across all your devices. When you complete a protocol, it automatically deducts from your stockpile!
2. **Auto-Reconstitution** 🔄 - When you reconstitute a peptide, The Pep Planner automatically adds it to your stockpile and creates your protocol tasks.
3. **Track Shipments** 📦 - Add tracking numbers to your orders. We'll monitor delivery status and notify you when peptides arrive.
4. **Set Research Goals** 🎯 - Track your research goals and bio-metrics over time. Perfect for monitoring protocol effectiveness.
5. **Budget Tracking** 💰 - Monitor your spending across vendors. The app tracks monthly and total costs automatically.
6. **Smart Reminders** 🔔 - Never miss a dose with research reminders! Get notified for low peptides in your stockpile or protocols that are supposed to be cycled soon!
7. **Compliance Streaks** 📊 - Build momentum! Your Research Consistency widget tracks your adherence streaks and completion rates.
8. **Vendor Management** 🏪 - Save your favorite vendors with notes and ratings. Track which peptides you get from each source.
9. **Consistent Scheduling** ⏰ - Stay on track with proper protocol timing! The app ensures your research follows consistent, protocol-based schedules for optimal results.
10. **Offline Mode** 📱 - The app works offline! Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.
11. **Research Glossary** 🔍 - Not sure what a term means? Use the glossary widget for instant peptide research definitions.
12. **Hydration Tracking** 💧 - Many peptides work better with proper hydration. Use the water tracker to monitor your daily intake.
13. **Achievement Badges** 🏆 - Earn badges for consistency milestones! Track your progress and celebrate your research journey.
14. **Quick Notes** 📝 - Jot down observations or side effects instantly with the Research Notes widget.
15. **Quick Actions** ⚡ - Use the Quick Actions widget to reconstitute, add orders, vendors, or protocols with one tap.
16. **Customize Dashboard** 🎨 - Tap the customize button to rearrange widgets, resize them, or hide ones you don't need.
17. **Secure & Private** 🔐 - Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your research information.
18. **Group Buys** 📅 - Schedule upcoming purchases in advance. Never forget to restock your peptides before running out!
19. **Stockpile Workflow** 💧 - Once your stockpile is set up, everything flows! Click the droplet icon to import vial details into the calculator, or select vials when creating protocols. Saving automatically updates your stock!
20. **Getting Started** 🚀 - New to the app? Start by importing your stockpile first - include what you already have reconstituted in your fridge. Then add some protocols, and everything else falls into place!
21. **Detailed Inventory Stats** 📊 - Track everything about your peptides! The app keeps detailed stats: vendor, purity, dates, even COAs to upload. Same peptide from multiple vendors? It tracks exactly how much you have from each!
22. **Precision Inventory** 🔗 - When you use the recon calculator or schedule protocols, pull directly from your stockpile. Everything syncs automatically - grab peptide A from vendor Z, and your inventory stays precise!
23. **Your Favorite Vendors** ⭐ - Vendors are always changing, but it's nice to have a spot for them - especially your favorites! Keep all your trusted sources organized in one place.
24. **Stop Over-Ordering** 🚫 - Get notified when peptides run low! Track all order details and vendors in one place. View any vendor to see all orders you've placed with them. No more over-ordering!
25. **Delivery Methods** 💉 - Using pens? The app tracks pen colors and types! Manage different delivery methods alongside your vials for complete research tracking.
26. **Washout Reminders** 🔄 - Taking breaks between cycles? Set washout reminders to notify you when it's time to restart. Perfect for protocols requiring rest periods between cycles.
27. **Supplement Tracking** 💊 - Track your complete supplement stack! The app manages your supplements alongside peptides, with schedules, adherence tracking, and reminders for your entire routine.
28. **Injection Site Rotation** 💉 - Track your injection sites and history for proper rotation. The app helps you avoid overusing the same spots and maintain healthy injection practices.
29. **Expiration Tracking** ⏳ - Monitor peptide expiration dates - both before and after reconstitution! Get notified before vials expire so nothing goes to waste.
30. **Dose Titration** 📈 - Adjust and track dose changes over time as you dial in your protocols. Perfect for finding your optimal dosing schedule.
31. **Protocol History** 📜 - View your past protocols to see what worked! Replicate successful cycles and learn from your research history.
32. **Data Export** 💾 - Backup your complete research data for safekeeping. Export everything to keep your records secure outside the app.
33. **Dark Mode** 🎨 - Switch between light and dark themes for comfortable viewing any time of day. Easy on the eyes during late-night research sessions!
34. **Search & Filter** 🔍 - Quickly find specific peptides, vendors, or protocols in your stockpile. No more scrolling through long lists!
35. **COA Uploads** 📸 - Attach Certificate of Analysis documents directly to your vials for easy reference. Keep all your quality verification in one place!
