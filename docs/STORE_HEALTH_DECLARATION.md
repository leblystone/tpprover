# Store ship checklist — Medications & Lab journal

When releasing the build that includes medications + blood/lab journaling:

## Google Play

1. Open Play Console → Policy → App content → **Health apps** declaration.
2. Declare applicable features accurately (medication journaling / health tracking as form options allow).
3. Keep store listing disclaimer: not a medical device; does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.
4. Align description copy with journal-only framing (no clinical claims).

## Apple App Store

1. Keep current category unless intentionally moving to Medical.
2. Description / review notes: personal research journal only; no diagnosis or lab interpretation.
3. Do not claim device-sensor medical measurements.
4. Existing Terms/Privacy disclaimers cover these journals.

## Product copy to avoid

- High/low/abnormal clinical flags
- Dose or treatment recommendations from labs
- Drug interaction checkers
- AI "what this lab means"
