## Store Listing Content

### Google Play Store
- [ ] **App Title**: "The Pep Planner"
- [ ] **Short Description**: 80 characters max
- [ ] **Full Description**: 4000 characters max
- [ ] **Keywords**: Comma-separated
- [ ] **Category**: Medical, Productivity, or Education
- [ ] **Content Rating**: Appropriate for your target audience

### App Store (iOS)
- [ ] **App Name**: "The Pep Planner"
- [ ] **Subtitle**: 30 characters max
- [ ] **Description**: Detailed feature list
- [ ] **Keywords**: 100 characters max
- [ ] **Category**: Medical, Productivity, or Education
- [ ] **Age Rating**: Appropriate for your target audience

## Additional Assets (Optional but Recommended)

### Marketing Materials
- [ ] **App Preview Video**: 30 seconds max (App Store)
- [ ] **Promotional Images**: For social media and marketing
- [ ] **Press Kit**: High-res logos and screenshots
- [ ] **Website**: Landing page for the app

### Legal and Compliance
- [ ] **Privacy Policy**: Required for both stores
- [ ] **Terms of Service**: Recommended
- [ ] **App Permissions**: List of required permissions
- [ ] **Data Usage**: How user data is handled

## Quality Assurance

### Screenshot Quality
- [ ] High resolution and crisp text
- [ ] Consistent theme across all screenshots
- [ ] Real data visible (no empty states)
- [ ] Key features highlighted
- [ ] Professional appearance
- [ ] No personal or sensitive information

### Content Quality
- [ ] Compelling and accurate descriptions
- [ ] Proper keyword optimization
- [ ] Clear value proposition
- [ ] Professional tone
- [ ] Research-focused messaging
- [ ] No medical advice claims

## Submission Process

### Google Play Store
1. [ ] Create Google Play Console account
2. [ ] Set up app listing
3. [ ] Upload assets and content
4. [ ] Configure app settings
5. [ ] Submit for review
6. [ ] Monitor review status

### App Store (iOS)
1. [ ] Create Apple Developer account
2. [ ] Set up app in App Store Connect
3. [ ] Upload assets and content
4. [ ] Configure app settings
5. [ ] Submit for review
6. [ ] Monitor review status
