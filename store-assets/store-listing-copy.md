### Full Description (4000 characters max)
```
The Pep Planner is a comprehensive research protocol management tool designed for peptide research and tracking. Whether you're conducting laboratory research, managing peptide protocols, or tracking reconstitution calculations, this app provides the organization and precision you need.

🧬 PROTOCOL MANAGEMENT
Create and manage detailed research protocols with comprehensive scheduling, dosing cycles, and timing controls. Track protocol adherence and completion history for consistent research practices.

🧮 RECONSTITUTION CALCULATOR
Precise peptide reconstitution calculations with support for various units (mg, mcg, mL). Calculate proper dosing, water ratios, and vial usage with built-in formulas and validation.

📦 ORDER TRACKING
Manage vendors and track orders from placement to delivery. Organize domestic, international, and group buy vendors with contact information, payment methods, and order history.

📊 INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
Track your stockpile with detailed peptide information, batch numbers, expiration dates, and usage tracking. Automatic sync from delivered orders to maintain accurate inventory records.

📅 CALENDAR INTEGRATION
Schedule and track research activities with integrated calendar functionality. View protocol schedules, upcoming orders, and important dates in one organized view.

🔒 SECURE & PRIVATE
All data is stored locally on your device with optional cloud backup. No data sharing or external servers - your research information stays private and secure.

🎨 CUSTOMIZABLE THEMES
Choose from multiple professional themes including Sage, Mauve, and Taupe to personalize your research environment.

KEY FEATURES:
• Protocol creation and management
• Reconstitution calculator with multiple units
• Vendor and order tracking
• Inventory management with expiration tracking
• Calendar integration and scheduling
• Secure local data storage
• Customizable themes and interface
• Export and sharing capabilities
• Offline functionality

Perfect for researchers, laboratories, and individuals conducting peptide research who need organized protocol management and precise calculations.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: This app is designed for research purposes only. All content is for informational and research use. Not intended for medical advice or human consumption.

Download The Pep Planner today and streamline your research protocol management!
```
